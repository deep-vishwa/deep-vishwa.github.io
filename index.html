01 / Research

Understanding bone.
Targeting disease.

I study how immune-cell signaling controls osteoclast formation and bone remodeling, and how these pathways can be targeted to address inflammatory and degenerative bone loss.

My work brings together osteoimmunology, conditional genetics, phenotypic screening and structure-based therapeutic discovery. At Wenzhou-Kean University, I founded the Laboratory of Osteoimmunology and Drug Discovery, establishing experimental and computational platforms for an independent research program.

Osteoclast signaling

RANKL-driven differentiation and the NF-κB, MAPK, NFATc1 and AKT/GSK-3β pathways that shape osteoclast function.

Genetics & bone physiology

Conditional knockout and lineage-tracing models to investigate myeloid-cell function, ciliary proteins and bone homeostasis.

Therapeutic discovery

Natural products and small molecules evaluated through cell-based screening, molecular docking, dynamics and experimental validation.

Immunity & mechanobiology

Complementary research on mast-cell MRGPRX2 signaling and osteocyte responses to mechanical stimulation under estrogen deficiency.
